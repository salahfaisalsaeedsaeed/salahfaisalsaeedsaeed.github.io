About

Academic and applied engineering profile

A concise overview of my background in mechatronics, robotics, technical education, and publication-oriented research practice.

Profile

I am a Mechatronics and Robotics Engineer with an applied and research-oriented profile at the intersection of computer science, robotics, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, computer vision, control systems, optimization, and laboratory-based STEM education.

My work combines hands-on engineering implementation, programming instruction, research mentorship, manuscript preparation, and technical training for students and teachers.

Education

  • B.Sc. in Engineering
    Mechatronics and Robotics Engineering, Taiz University, Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology, 2025.
  • Vocational Professional Diploma
    Industrial Control Systems / Electronics, Technical Industrial Institute, Sana’a, Yemen.

Current Direction

My current research direction includes UAV control, PV-integrated bidirectional EV fast charging, off-grid hybrid renewable energy system optimization, federated learning, cybersecurity, security digital twins, and AI-assisted engineering analysis.
