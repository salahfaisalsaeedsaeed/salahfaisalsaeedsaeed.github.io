Biography

Engineering, research, and technical education

Full profile →

Salah Faisal Saeed Saeed is a Mechatronics and Robotics Engineer whose academic and professional work combines engineering practice, laboratory development, technical education, and applied research. His background includes robotics, electronics, embedded systems, instrumentation, computer vision, control systems, engineering simulation, and optimization.

His professional experience also includes physics and STEM laboratory practice, technical training, equipment preparation and troubleshooting, student project guidance, and research mentorship. He has contributed to peer-reviewed IEEE conference publications and has supported students in scientific research, academic writing, and publication-oriented work.
