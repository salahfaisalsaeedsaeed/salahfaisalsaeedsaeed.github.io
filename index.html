Biography

Engineering, research, and technical education

Full profile →

Salah Faisal is a Mechatronics and Robotics Engineer whose work combines engineering practice, technical instruction, laboratory development, scientific writing, and collaborative research. His academic and applied interests span robotics, computer vision, intelligent control, embedded systems, energy systems, optimization, and trustworthy artificial intelligence.

Current research activity includes UAV control, PV-integrated bidirectional EV fast charging, hybrid renewable-energy optimization, federated learning, cybersecurity, security digital twins, and AI-assisted engineering analysis.
