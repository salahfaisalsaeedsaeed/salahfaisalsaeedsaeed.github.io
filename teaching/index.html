Experience

Teaching & Mentorship

Physics/STEM instruction, laboratory training, teacher professional development, technical education, and student research mentorship.

Teaching and mentorship combine Grade 11 physics, laboratory experiments, practical electronics, measurement, embedded systems, robotics, engineering simulation, technical projects, and structured research training. The research-mentorship pathway progresses from research fundamentals and academic writing to manuscript preparation and publication-oriented work.

Research-to-publication mentorshipThe annual Engineering Research and Academic Writing Training Program began in 2024 and runs from August to April. The 2024–2025 cycle led to two student-involved IEEE Xplore publications, and the 2025–2026 cycle led to four.
