About

About

Academic, engineering, and professional profile.

Salah Faisal Saeed Saeed is a Mechatronics and Robotics Engineer with a multidisciplinary academic and applied background in robotics, embedded systems, electronics, instrumentation, computer vision, MATLAB/Simulink-based modeling, control systems, optimization, and STEM technical education. He holds a B.Sc. in Mechatronics and Robotics Engineering from Taiz University and a Vocational Professional Diploma in Industrial Control Systems and Electronics.

His professional experience combines laboratory engineering, technical instruction, equipment preparation and troubleshooting, student project guidance, research mentorship, academic writing, and collaborative publication activity. He has contributed to peer-reviewed IEEE conference publications and supported structured student research and publication-oriented academic training.

His current research work spans intelligent control and robotics, EV fast-charging systems, renewable-energy optimization, federated learning, trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, security digital twins, and AI-assisted technical analysis.
